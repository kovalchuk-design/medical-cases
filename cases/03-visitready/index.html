Problem

The front desk is the bottleneck every patient passes through

  • On-site registration — paper forms, a copy of the insurance card, ID, signatures, copay — creates a line and makes the first appointment start late.
  • Registration errors (wrong member ID, inactive coverage) lead to denied claims and billing rework.
  • No-show averages about 23% (Dantas et al., 2018). Rescheduling is hard, so patients simply don't come.
  • Telehealth visits fail at the start because of camera, microphone and connection issues.

Product story

Bottleneck → hypothesis → solution

Bottleneck

Registration at the door: every minute at the front desk multiplies across every morning patient.

Hypothesis

If 70% of patients check in ahead of time, the line disappears, the first visit starts on time, and OCR + eligibility checks cut registration errors.

Solution

Pre-visit check-in (card OCR, eligibility, forms prefilled from the last visit, e-sign, payment), "I'm here", one-tap reschedule, telehealth tech check.

Compliance & accessibility

Safe by default

HIPAA

PHI only after authentication. SMS links are short-lived with OTP sign-in. No diagnoses in notifications — "Room 4 is ready" only.

Patient safety

Two identifiers (full name + date of birth) before any PHI changes. Proxy flows always name the patient, not the account holder.

Payments & ID

PCI DSS via the payment provider; card numbers are never stored. ID photo encrypted, with the purpose stated.

WCAG 2.2 AA

Text ≥ 4.5:1, targets ≥ 44 pt, Dynamic Type to XXL, VoiceOver cues for the scanner, "Type my name" as an alternative to drawing a signature.
