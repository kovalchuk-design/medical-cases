People
Three fictional personas built around the public research. They make trade-offs concrete.
MC
Dr. Maya Chen, 41
Anesthesiologist · Austin, TX
Persona · Primary
Permanent hospital job plus one or two locum blocks a quarter. Licensed in Texas and California.
Goal
$300+/hr without haggling and no surprise malpractice gaps.
Frustration
Can’t tell if an offer is fair until after she says yes; re-sends the same eleven documents to every hospital.
Needs
•
Take-home before she replies
•
Terms flagged in plain language
•
Under two hours a month on paperwork
JR
Jordan Reyes, RN, 29
ICU travel & per diem nurse · Phoenix, AZ
Persona · Secondary
Holds a multistate compact license and picks up shifts on three apps between travel contracts.
Goal
Shifts that are paid even if cancelled late, and one calendar across every app.
Frustration
Rates drop when more nurses are online; cancelled shifts with no pay and no one to call.
Needs
•
A going rate she can see
•
A cancellation guarantee
•
Conflicts caught before booking
