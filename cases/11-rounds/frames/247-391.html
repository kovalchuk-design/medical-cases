Brief
Problem
About 50,000 US physicians — plus NPs, PAs, CRNAs and nurses — take temporary assignments every year. Each evaluates offers, negotiates rates and terms, re-submits the same credentials and reconciles 1099 income largely alone, while the intermediaries they rely on are paid by facilities.
Role
Product designer, end to end: research, UX, UI, prototype.
Platform
iOS 26 on iPhone · English, US, USD · Light and Dark tokens.
Scope
4 journeys, 10 areas, 157 screens including edge cases, errors and empty states.
Timeline
6 weeks: 2 discover and define, 3 design, 1 prototype and validation plan.
Inside the file
Research & Strategy · Style guide · Icons · Components · 4 journey pages · Play-mode case presentation.
