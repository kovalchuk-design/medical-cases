9
Exceptions
Values that are intentionally not bound to Rounds variables — and why. Everything else is bound to a variable or a style.
Rule
Where
Why
Apple iOS 26 kit components
Status bar, Toolbar, Sheet toolbar, Segmented control, Toggle base, Progress bar
Use the library’s own tokens. Only our overrides are bound (toggle on = bg/brand, progress fill = bg/inverse).
Data-driven geometry
Chart bar Column padding, histogram bar heights, benchmark marker positions
Values encode data, not layout.
Absolute positions
Unread dot on the bell, scrims and photos inside cards
Position, not spacing; sizes follow the parent.
Image fills
Photos, avatars, facility thumbnails
Images are content; their containers use radius tokens.
Scaled brand instances
Logo mark on Splash, Cover and in the style guide
Instance scale only; colours remain bound.
Canvas organisation
Sections and component-set frames
Not part of the UI.
