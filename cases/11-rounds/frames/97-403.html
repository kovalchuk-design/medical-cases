Usage rules
Lime = active & actionable
Primary button, selected tab, toggle on, highlight pill, agent mark.
Never for body text or large backgrounds behind text.
Black = strong & selected
Secondary standalone action, selected chips, ask marker, safe choice in destructive dialogs.
Status colours carry meaning
Success, warning, danger, info only for state: verified, expiring, missing, risk. Never decorative.
Text on photos
Always over an overlay/image-* scrim; use text/on-image and text/on-image-accent. Contrast ≥ 4.5:1 on the darkest stop.
