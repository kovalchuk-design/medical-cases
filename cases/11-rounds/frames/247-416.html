02
Process
Five phases, each with the one thing it produced.
01
Discover
Desk research across industry surveys, policy reports and pricing guides; a landscape of intermediaries by business model.
02
Define
Eight problems ranked by severity × frequency, three personas, a journey and five principles for agent behaviour.
03
Design
Information architecture, a token-based system on Apple’s iOS 26 kit and 157 screens covering every edge case and state.
04
Prototype
A Play-mode case with three journeys, animated transitions and keyboard navigation.
05
Validate
Moderated sessions with 8–12 clinicians on the hero flows, plus 2–3 expert interviews. Success: 80% unaided task completion, SUS 75+.
