How Rounds speaks. Clear, specific, warm — never salesy.
Rule
Example
Note
Agent speaks in first person
“I’ll ask for occurrence-based cover.”
Never “Rounds AI has determined…”.
Lead with the conclusion, then the reason
“Ask for $320/hr” → “Anesthesiologists booked $305–$340/hr in Central TX.”
One reason, one line.
Money: US format, weight for emphasis
$320/hr · $2,950/day · $18,420
No colour for money; −$9.60 uses a true minus.
Dates & times
Jan 12 – 30 · Dec 20 · 9:41 AM · Today
En dash with spaces for ranges.
Buttons are verbs
Review & negotiate · Send counter-offer · Upload TB test
Max 3 words; no “OK”/“Submit”.
Sentence case everywhere
Things to do · Credential wallet
Except proper names and acronyms (NPI, DEA, TB).
State the consequence before commitment
“Nothing is binding until you sign.”
Shown above any Agree / Send action.
Respect privacy
“Shared only with St. Mary’s.”
Say who sees a document every time one is shared.
