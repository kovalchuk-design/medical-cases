Business and risks
Business model
Clinician-paid, flat 3%
Shown as its own line in every rate breakdown (2.3.1). The fee only grows when the clinician’s take-home grows, which keeps the agent aligned with the person it serves.
Later: tools that help facilities receive clean, complete credentialing files. Never paid placement in the feed.
Risk
Mitigation
Bad advice from the agent
Every recommendation shows its data and confidence; clause flags explain rather than advise; legal and tax questions go to a professional
Facilities won’t deal with an agent
Messages go out as the clinician, with clear disclosure; the clinician can take over any thread
Sensitive personal data
Credentials encrypted; every share is per facility, consented and revocable (4.3.7); no patient data in scope
Staffing-platform regulation
Rounds doesn’t employ or place clinicians; fees and agent actions are disclosed by design
Thin data in small markets
Show ranges and sample size; say “not enough data” instead of guessing
