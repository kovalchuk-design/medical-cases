Rules
Money is emphasised by weight
Amounts inside text take the sibling style one weight up: Body/Regular → Body/Medium, Subheadline/Regular → Subheadline/Semibold, Footnote/Regular → Footnote/Semibold. Never by colour.
Minimum sizes
11 px only for tab labels; 12 px for any other meta text. Body text never below 14 px.
Brand styles are not UI
Brand/Wordmark and Brand/Hero are reserved for the logo and marketing surfaces.
