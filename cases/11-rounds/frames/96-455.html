Rounds · Design system v1.0
Style guide
The single source of truth for Rounds. Every value in the product file is bound to the variables and styles described here — no hard-coded colours, sizes or spacing.
Principles
The agent works for the clinician
Every recommendation shows its reason. The agent never signs, accepts or shares documents without Face ID.
Money first, plainly
Rates, totals and fees are the decision points — emphasised by weight, explained in one line, never hidden.
One focus per screen
One primary action. Supporting detail sits behind ⓘ or a row, not on the surface.
Calm, clinical, warm
Soft neutrals and generous radii; lime only where something is active or actionable.
